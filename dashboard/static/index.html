About power a2z

This dashboard reads your local power_a2z/data/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl files. New complete samples appear automatically every 10 seconds.

Power, voltage, and energy are converted from the kernel's micro-units into watts, volts, and watt-hours. Battery power is the magnitude measured at the primary system battery, including during charging; it does not represent wall-socket consumption.

Runtime is a snapshot estimate from remaining energy and current power. Charging shows time to full. Energy discharged uses trapezoidal integration and excludes collection gaps longer than 60 seconds or three sampling intervals.

Time windows end at the latest recorded sample. Chart times use your browser's timezone. Missing measurements appear as a dash. Health can exceed 100% when a battery's full capacity is above its design rating.

Thermals include hwmon sensors and thermal zones. Sensor limits come from the hardware when available. Fan speed shows the fastest reported fan; zero RPM means stopped, while missing, failed, or stale readings appear as a dash.
